Meaning of Life
What's the meaning of life?
Bitterness of coffee,
or the sweet after taste.
the gentleness of milk foam
After days floating on the sailing boat,
with a sense of direction,
but the trajectory in the hands of wind,
isn't it life?
we know where we are going,
we control the steering wheel, our sails,
but we wait for wind,
borrow the force of wind.
it's about going somewhere,
but about patience, strategy.

Under the Stars
Under the stars,
you kissed me.
Moon-lit sky,
summery night,
How long does time last?
Time is stretched out in the deep blue sky,
into the universe,
and beyond the edge of what we know,
time stops to matter.
the world, as we know it, stops making sense,
for the moment then,
then & then,
it seems to be the meaning of everything.
We feel alive
in your arms,
along your heart beat
Is this real, is love real.

Montana Land
Past, present, future,
those are terms threaded by time
Here, now, there, then,
spaces and distances are only relative
Some days, months, years
pass by in a haze.
Whilst other seconds of moments,
we hold on through eternity.
Our past brought you and me
together, here, now, in this present moment.
[Pause]
One, two, three
[Breathe in, Breathe out]
Can we observe those moments of entanglement
that alters the course of our paths forward?
Is an "ending" necessary for time to make sense?
Is pain necessary for us to love?
Are wars necessary for us to make peace?
Mistakes change the course of civilizations.
Imperfections undermines humanity.
Are regrets and the lack of second chances
what makes life worth living?
With courage and compassion.
Time seems to go on.
Cry, river, cry,
let it nourish the soul and wash away those scars
Tomorrow, the sun rises again,
on this vast Montana Land.

Dancing in the Blue
There is nothing but blue,
Deep and endless blue.
No boundaries, borders, shapes, or time.
Some rays of sunlight
attempts to penetrate,
but gave up and got lost in the blue.
An irresistible invitation
to the endless unknown,
danger, adventure.
Though I feel a sense of fear towards the unknown,
mixed with danger, adventure, challenge.
Probably there is nothing.
It's me having a monologue with my emotions.
The Blue seems undisturbed at all.
To keep going down,
how long can my breath hold?
Beneath me, it's death, it's the Blue, quietness, and the end of time.
Turning back,
it's life, light,
it's the world as I know it.
How lonely it is to be a fish,
with 3 dimensions of freedom,
with the freedom comes loneliness and quietness.
Swimming around,
no sounds, no conversations,
just meditation with the Blue.
For the fish, the land is a place of joy, he heard,
full of entertainment, to rid the loneliness.
One day,
he gave up one dimension of freedom,
and learned to walk and talk,
at first with clumsy feet and clumsy mouth.
But over the years,
he perfected his skills and even learned to dance on land!
The fish earned a respectable position in society.
He has made it! And he is proud!
He made a family, living in a big house.
One day, on a lazy afternoon when he took his son fishing,
his son asked: "where did you come from daddy?"
He looked at the sea and hesitated.
"A far place, son."
The fish said no more.
That night, he had a dream,
and he was dancing again in the Blue.

Hope
I see ruins, and darkness.
Some scattered light — faint, but newborn.
They are hope.
They will ignite.
The stars above guide them,
born of the past and the future —

peaceful music drifts and spreads.

The Love Story of Fire and Ice
He cradled a burning,
pulsing heart in his palms,
to warm my heart of ice.
Ice and fire melted into each other.
My icy heart gazed upon the heart beating for me—
A crack opened in the ice.
Warm tears streamed from the crack.
An awakened heart began to beat.
That heart had been awake all along—
it lacked the courage to shatter its icy shield.
By then, the fire had gone out.

Birds are Singing
My heart is beating,
within it, you.
Tomorrow, maybe we meet again.
Maybe not.
For the first time since you left, I smiled.
I wonder how you are, my dear—
if I still linger in your heart,
if the wounds I left behind have healed,
and whether if they've scarred.
I feel you.
So you must feel me too.
Somewhere in another dimension,
You must be right beside me.
